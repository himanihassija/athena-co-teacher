# Athena EchoSphere — Voiceover Script (Fast Cut)

*Short sentences. No lingering. Say it like you already know it's good.*

---

**[Loading screen]**

> "My mother's a teacher. Thirty kids. A dozen decisions a minute. Alone."

> "We built her a co-teacher."

---

**[Athena joins — orb appears, "Hi everyone,"]**

> "Meet Athena. Not a chatbot. A voice, in the room, live — running on Agora's Conversational AI Engine. Speech, reasoning, voice — one pipeline, real time, no patchwork."

**[Mrs. Rao: "we'll be studying photosynthesis today"]**

> "Class starts. Athena doesn't speak. She listens. Because the hardest skill for an AI in a classroom isn't talking. It's knowing when not to."

---

**["Athena, could you explain what photosynthesis is?"]**

> "Then she's asked. And she delivers."

**[explanation streams in]**

> "Light. Chlorophyll. Carbon dioxide. Glucose. Generated live. Not memorized — built, on the spot, in language a kid actually gets."

---

**["Draw flow of photosynthesis." → diagram appears]**

> "Tell us isn't enough. So Mrs. Rao says: show us."

> "Athena draws. Live. On a board every student sees. Five steps. In order. Correct."

> "A co-teacher with a hand. Not just a voice."

---

**[Student view — AI Assistant, 1:1 Connect, Raise Hand]**

> "Every student's got their own room. Their own line to Athena. Their own way to reach the teacher — without stopping the class."

**[Live quiz, timer running]**

> "And Athena checks comprehension live. Mid-lesson. Ten seconds on the clock. No waiting for a test to find out who's lost."

---

**[Nobody Left Behind — sticky notes, 1:1 booking]**

> "Every teacher knows this problem. Who's missing. Who's falling behind, quietly. We named it: Nobody Left Behind."

> "Live doubts, pinned for the whole class. Real 1:1 slots, booked in seconds — pick a time, name the topic, done."

**[Gmail — real email delivered]**

> "Miss class? A parent gets notified. Automatically. Summary, takeaways, a catch-up quiz — written and sent before the teacher even leaves the room."

*(hold this shot — it's real. Not a mockup.)*

---

**[Header: Mute AI / Unmute AI / Send Athena out]**

> "Here's the part that matters most."

> "One click. Mute her. Unmute her. Send her out — no explanation owed."

> "The AI doesn't run this classroom. The teacher does. Athena just answers to her."

---

**["Athena AI Assistant" — teacher-only panel]**

> "There's a second Athena too. Private. Teacher-only. Check-in questions, real-world analogies, a board problem drafted in seconds — without ever breaking the lesson."

---

**[Post-class report — 100% grasp, gaps, per-student data]**

> "Class ends. Nothing's lost. Full report — what landed, what didn't, student by student. Not a guess. A record."

---

**[Closing]**

> "She listens. She explains. She draws. She checks who got it. She waits her turn — and she steps back the second she's told to."

> "Built on Agora's real-time infrastructure."

> "Not an AI running a classroom. A teacher, finally handed something built for her."

> "Funny thing — when it was time to pick a problem worth solving, almost nobody picked hers."

> "We did."

---

### Notes

- Delivery is clipped, declarative, confident — almost every line can stand alone. Say each one like a statement, not a lead-in to the next.
- Cut points if you need it shorter: the student-view/quiz beat and the teacher-copilot beat are the two safest to drop entirely — everything else is load-bearing.
- Don't add "and" or "so" between beats when reading — let each line land, then move. The gaps are doing the pacing, not filler words.
